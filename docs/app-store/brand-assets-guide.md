# ProfitPilot brand assets guide

## Icon

- Square **1024 × 1024 px** master; export 512 × 512 and 256 × 256 variants.
- PNG or SVG source; transparent background for the icon variant.
- Use the ProfitPilot blue-to-violet mark on a dark, high-contrast field.
- Do not add Shopify, OpenAI, Google, or other partner marks to the app icon.
- Keep clear space equal to at least 12% of the canvas around the mark.

## Logo

- Horizontal SVG/PNG lockup with the mark and `ProfitPilot` wordmark.
- Minimum digital width: 160 px; never stretch, rotate, add effects, or change the wordmark spelling.
- Provide dark-background and light-background exports.

## Color and accessibility

- Primary blue: `#3B82F6`; bright focus blue: `#72A7FF`.
- Use text and interactive states that meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; verify every final asset against its background.
- Keep focus rings and keyboard-visible states intact in screenshots.

## Source-of-truth and licensing

Keep editable source files outside the repository unless they are intentionally approved product assets. Record font, icon, illustration, and photograph licenses. Do not include merchant or customer information in brand exports.
