# Shopify App Store category selection guide

Select categories based on the capabilities that are live in the submitted build, not on future F8/F9 roadmap items.

## Recommended primary category

**Store management** — ProfitPilot's live core is tenant-scoped store monitoring, deterministic insights, approvals, sync, and operational automation.

## Optional secondary category

**Marketing and merchandising** — select this only when the submitted Shopify scopes, campaign controls, suppression ledger, verified merchant sender, and compliance footer are enabled in the review environment.

## Do not select yet

Do not market ProfitPilot as a forecasting, voice, fully autonomous purchasing, or guaranteed-profit app until those capabilities are implemented, tested, and no longer phase-gated. Do not claim that it makes decisions without merchant review.

## Evidence for review

Keep a short evidence pack for each selected category: live route, permission scope, screenshot name, deterministic data source, approval requirement, and test command. The evidence pack must contain no customer personal data and must be reproducible from the submitted build.
